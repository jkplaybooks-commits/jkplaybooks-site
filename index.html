FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is JK Playbooks?+

A practical learning library focused on digital skills, AI workflows, tools and implementation playbooks.

Is JK Playbooks a course?+

It is designed as a growing library of focused playbooks, tutorials, resources and workflows rather than one long course.

Is it suitable for beginners?+

Many resources are beginner-friendly. Advanced topics may require some familiarity with the relevant tools.

Do I need advanced technical skills?+

No. The focus is on practical steps. Any advanced requirements will be stated in the relevant resource.

How often are new playbooks added?+

New resources can be added regularly as new tools, workflows and practical topics are developed.

Can I use the playbooks for my own projects?+

Yes, subject to the applicable terms and any license restrictions for individual resources.

Are the tools included with membership?+

Membership includes JK Playbooks resources and recommendations. Some third-party tools may have separate pricing.

Can I cancel my membership?+

Cancellation can be handled through the payment or membership system used for your subscription. Check the applicable billing terms.

Does JK Playbooks guarantee income?+

No. JK Playbooks provides educational resources. Results depend on execution, experience, market conditions and other factors.

Can I suggest a new playbook topic?+

Yes. Send your suggestion through WhatsApp or email and we can consider it for the library.
