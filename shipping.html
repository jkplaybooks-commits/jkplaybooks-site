Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. Digital Products Only

JK Playbooks sells digital products only — playbooks (e-books), templates, workflows, video tutorials and community membership. No physical goods are shipped, so there are no shipping charges and no physical delivery.

2. Delivery Method & Timing

"Delivery" means granting digital access:

  • Community membership (WhatsApp/Telegram): access is granted within 24 hours of successful payment, usually much sooner.
  • E-books, playbooks and templates: download links or files are shared via WhatsApp/email/community within 24 hours of payment.

3. If Access Is Not Delivered

If you paid but have not received access within 24 hours, contact us at jkplaybooks@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91-9789361174. Access will be restored immediately, or the payment will be refunded in full (see our Cancellation & Refund Policy).

4. Updates to Content

When we update a playbook or resource you have access to, the new version is shared in the community at no additional cost.

5. International Members

International customers ($11.90/month) receive the same digital access. Delivery is unaffected by location.

6. Contact

Email jkplaybooks@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91-9789361174.
