Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. Cancellation of Membership

There is no lock-in period. You may cancel your JK Playbooks membership at any time by simply not paying the next month's payment, or by contacting us on WhatsApp or email. Access continues until the end of the month you have already paid for.

2. Refunds — General

Because our products are digital and delivered instantly (e-books, playbooks, templates and community access), payments are generally non-refundable once access has been delivered.

3. Refund Exceptions

A full refund will be issued in the following cases, if reported within 7 days of payment:

  • You were charged twice for the same month.
  • Payment succeeded but you never received access to the content.
  • Access was delivered in error or materially different from what was described at purchase.

4. How to Request a Refund

Email jkplaybooks@gmail.com or message WhatsApp +91-9789361174 with your payment reference/UTR number and the reason for your request. We respond within 48 hours.

5. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed through Razorpay to the original payment method within 5–7 business days. Bank processing times may apply.

6. Chargebacks

We ask that you contact us before raising a chargeback — most issues can be resolved directly and quickly.

7. Contact
